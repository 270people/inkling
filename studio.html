Your plan (from the Design step)
3D preview
Drag the tattoo to move it on the skin. Drag anywhere else to turn the body. Mouse wheel to zoom in and out. Right-drag (or Shift-drag) to look higher or lower. Double-click to go back to the back view. The tattoo goes on like a sticker: same size and shape as your plan, bent over the body. This is only a preview; the stencil is made from your flat design in the next step.

Client photo

Make the 3D body look like your client. Turn on matching to see the photo behind a see-through body, at real size. Then move the Body sliders (start with Height) until the outline sits on your client. Use Line up to slide the photo up or down; the tattoo moves with it, so it lands where you planned it.
