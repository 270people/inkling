Inkling stencil test sheet

One page that tests your own printer, stencil paper and transfer solution, so Inkling can use real numbers instead of guesses.
How to use it
  1. Pick your printer's resolution (if unsure, 203 dpi).
  2. If you printed this sheet before and the 100 mm ruler came out a different length, type what it measured (for example 90). The sheet is then made smaller or bigger so your printer app prints it at true size.
  3. Download the PNG and print it from your tattoo printer app. Some printer apps turn PDFs into a blurry picture, so PNG is safer.
  4. Measure both 100 mm rulers with a real ruler before putting it on skin.
  5. Transfer it to practice skin or your forearm as you normally would. Take photos right away, after 15 minutes, and after wiping 10 times.
